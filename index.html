How scoring works

Points accuracy: each team is worth up to 10 points. You lose 1 point for every point you're off, down to a minimum of 0.

Position bonus: +10 for each team you place in its exact spot in its division.

Perfect entry: 20 per team, 640 in total.

Valid totals: the league hands out 2 points per regulation game and 3 per overtime game. Across 1,344 games, that puts the season total between 2,688 and 4,032 points.
